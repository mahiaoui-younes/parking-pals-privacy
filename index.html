Parking Pals: Colorful Escape

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: Parking Pals collects no personal information at all. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline.

Who this game is for

Parking Pals is a puzzle game designed for children aged 6 to 8. It is built to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and the GDPR (European Union).

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or device information. It does not use advertising identifiers, location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, or any other personal data.

Information stored on the device

To remember game progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on the device itself: which levels were completed, stars earned, the chosen car decoration, and the music, sound and language settings. This data never leaves the device. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled, and it can be reset at any time in Settings → For parents (behind a parental check).

Internet access

The app does not request permission to access the internet and does not send any data anywhere.

Third-party services

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking services.

Parental controls

The parents' area is protected by a parental gate (a question an adult must answer). The game contains no links to websites, no purchases and no social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. We will never begin collecting children's personal information without verifiable parental consent as required by law.
